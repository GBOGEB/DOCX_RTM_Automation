**General Tips for the Systematic Approach:**

* **Version Control (Git):** Commit frequently with clear messages after each small, successful change. This allows you to revert if something breaks and helps track progress. Use branches for significant new features or refactoring.
* **One Thing at a Time:** Focus on fixing one script or one part of the pipeline before moving to the next.
* **Read Error Messages Carefully:** They often provide precise clues about the problem.
* **Simplify:** If a script is too complex, try to break it down into smaller functions or modules.
* **Documentation/Comments:** As you fix or understand code, add comments to explain complex parts or decisions.
